How the Refresher Works

Ten to fifteen minutes, a few times a week, keeps a finished track from fading while you work on the next one. It works by making you pull each idea back out of memory, which is what makes it stick.

Mixed
One set pulls from every track you include, so ideas from different tracks sit side by side.
Spaced
Get one right and it waits longer each time: 3, 7, 14, then 30 days. Miss it and it is back tomorrow.
Fresh
New questions, not the session quizzes again, so you recall the idea rather than recognise a question.
↗
Every answer links to the exact concept in the session that teaches it.
